(03) About

The person

I grew up in my father's photo studio and started designing at 12. Today I am the only designer for a global team serving 126 agencies.

Six years across a three-year freelance practice with 40+ clients, a startup where I was the first designer, VVDN Technologies where I art directed 100+ international exhibitions and wrote the brand guidelines, and Publicis Groupe, where I started the internal newsletter that now reaches 100,000+ employees.

Every project starts with the brief and the business problem behind it. AI and automation are how I get more of that work shipped without dropping the craft.

Outside work I sing, make content and still shoot photographs.
